CONTROLS

How to explore

You can explore the entire museum using only your mouse. Keyboard controls are optional.

MOVE MOUSE Look around
HOLD LEFT BUTTON Walk forward in the direction you are looking
HOLD RIGHT BUTTON Walk backward
CLICK OBJECT Open an artwork or use a screen
DOUBLE CLICK DOOR Enter a room or leave it
W A S D Optional alternative to mouse movement
SHIFT Walk faster
Q / E Turn left / right with the keyboard
BACKSPACE Go back to the previous place
SCROLL Zoom in and out
ESC Close an open window
RIGHT SIDE SWIPE Look around on mobile
LEFT JOYSTICK Move forward, backward and sideways
TAP Open artworks. Tap a door twice to enter or leave
PINCH Zoom in and out
